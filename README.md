A one-line roast of a phone battery: the phone keeps outliving the chargers
and power banks meant to keep it alive. The life support dies first — and
the phone, stubbornly, just keeps going.

`main.py` gates the line (UTF-8, 1–200 characters, not whitespace-only) and
writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, verifying the read-back
byte-for-byte.
